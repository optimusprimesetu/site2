Grammar

Scheduling uses Anki's SM-2 rules with its default deck options: learning steps 1m 10m, graduating interval 1 day, starting ease 250%, relearning step 10m, lapse ease −20%, 4 am day rollover, 20-minute learn-ahead, interval fuzz. You're graded by voice only: said right = Good, anything else = Again. Progress is saved in this browser. Progress bar: Seen = still in learning steps, Good = graduated, Mastered = interval of 21 days or more (Anki's “mature”).

Grammar: answer by saying the option (e.g. 「を」 or 「かいて」), its number (「いちばん」, 「2」, "three"), or tap it. Keys 1–4 also work. Sentences are picked to use the words you already know.

Pictures come from Wikipedia, Openverse and Wikimedia Commons unless you add a Google Custom Search key and engine ID. Space pauses.
